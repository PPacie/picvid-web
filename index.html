FAQs

1. How can I create videos?

You can add an image and/or type a prompt to create a video. When adding an image the prompt is optional.

2. How are the images converted?

We make use of AI models to create a video from your image and/or text.

3. Where do my video files go?

They're saved in the Videos tab inside the app. From there, you can export them anywhere. You might want to create your videos in Portrait (9:16) for them to be optimized for TikTok, Reels, and Shorts.

4. Can I save directly to my Photos app?

Yes, you can tap on “Save“ button.

5. Why is the Create button disabled?

You need to either add an image or type a prompt to create a video.

6. Can I select the aspect ratio of a video?

Yes, only when the video is created from a prompt (landscape, portrait, square). If the video is created from an image it will automatically take the same aspect of the selected image.

7. Is my data stored?

Videos you save in PicVid are stored on your device. To create a video, the image and/or prompt you submit is sent to third-party AI services for safety checks and video creation. We do not keep a PicVid user media database. See our Privacy Policy for more information.

8. Can I delete files from the Videos tab?

Yes. Tap “Select”, choose the files you want to remove, and tap Delete.

9. What images and prompts are allowed?

Use content you have the right to use. Everyday scenes are generally allowed. Do not submit images or prompts that contain—or ask us to create—objectionable content under Apple’s App Review Guidelines (e.g., nudity, graphic violence, targeted abuse, illegal acts, etc.). We consider the image and prompt together, so context matters.

10. Why was my request rejected?

Before creating a video, we check the image and prompt to help prevent objectionable content under Apple’s App Review Guidelines. If the request appears to contain prohibited content, or we cannot confidently determine that it is safe, we stop it and show an error. If you think we got it wrong, contact support. Flagged requests may receive further review, as explained in our Privacy Policy.
